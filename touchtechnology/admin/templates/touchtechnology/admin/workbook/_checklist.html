{% load i18n admin_workbook %}{% comment %} A checklist: give it ``checklist`` (a ``Setup``), ``title`` and ``kind``, the name the browser remembers its fold by ("season", "division"). The fold. The server folds it to its progress bar when every required step is done (``Setup.folded``) and opens it otherwise, so it works without scripts. With scripts, it also starts folded on a phone, and a fold the person chose wins over both: a click on the summary keeps it in localStorage as ``wb-checklist-fold:`` ("open" or "folded"), one choice for every checklist of that kind. ``wbChecklist`` (defined in the page's head) applies it as soon as the panel is parsed; until it has, the stylesheet keeps the steps out of sight, so an open panel never shows only to fold. Hiding. "Hide" puts every set-up checklist away: localStorage ``wb-checklists`` is "hidden", and the script in the page's head marks the page with it before anything is drawn. The page head's button (see ``_checklist_show.html``) brings them back. {% endcomment %}
